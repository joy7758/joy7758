# Reviewer Selection Worksheet

This worksheet is for human completion in the submission system. It does not
propose names.

## Suggested Reviewer Profile Types

- Software engineering researchers with expertise in software validation,
  verification, or artifact-centered evaluation
- Researchers familiar with provenance models, provenance constraints, or
  machine-readable audit records
- Researchers working on reproducibility, research artifacts, or executable
  software evidence
- Researchers with background in structured metadata, digital objects, or
  machine-actionable research objects
- Researchers who can assess bounded tools and methods without expecting a
  deployment-scale empirical systems paper

## Exclusion Profile Types

- Close collaborators, recent coauthors, or current institutional associates
- People with direct conflicts related to the underlying artifact repository or
  manuscript preparation
- Reviewers whose expertise is exclusively in unrelated adjacent areas and not
  in software validation, provenance, artifacts, or reviewable research objects
- Anyone whose involvement could create a perceived conflict of interest

## Conflict-Of-Interest Reminders

- Check recent coauthorship relationships
- Check current and recent institutional affiliations
- Check advisor/advisee and lab-level relationships if relevant
- Check funding, project, or direct collaboration conflicts
- Check any personal or professional conflict that the venue would expect to be
  disclosed

## Expertise The Manuscript Needs

- Bounded software validation and verification
- Structured provenance and constraint-based checking
- Artifact evaluation and reproducibility methodology
- Software engineering methods for inspectable tools and workflows
- Critical assessment of narrow, reviewer-facing research artifacts
