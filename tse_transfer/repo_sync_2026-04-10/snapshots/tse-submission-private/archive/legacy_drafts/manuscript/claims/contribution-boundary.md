## Allowed Contribution Framing

The current manuscript may present three bounded contributions:

1. A minimal profile named `Execution Evidence and Operation Accountability Profile v0.1` for one operation accountability statement.
2. A profile-aware validator path exposed through `agent-evidence validate-profile <file>` and described through `validation report` output.
3. A reproducible artifact package consisting of controlled examples, a single-path demo, and status/acceptance handoff materials.

## Boundary Conditions

- The contribution is artifact-centric, not platform-centric.
- The contribution is method-centric, not policy-theory-centric.
- The contribution is limited to the repository-validated path already documented in the public package.
- The contribution is about bounded independent verification, not broad operational assurance.

## Not Allowed As Contribution Claims

- A general registry architecture
- A complete governance framework
- A deployment study
- A multi-agent accountability system
- A complete FDO interoperability layer
- A full cryptographic trust fabric
- Formal guarantees beyond the documented validation checks
