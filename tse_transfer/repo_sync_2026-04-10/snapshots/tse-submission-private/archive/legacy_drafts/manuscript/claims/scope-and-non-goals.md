## In Scope

- One `operation accountability statement`
- One minimal profile identity and schema-constrained JSON structure
- One validator path exposed as `agent-evidence validate-profile <file>`
- One valid example
- Three invalid examples with one primary failure each
- One single-path metadata enrichment demo
- One bounded independent verification narrative grounded in repository evidence

## Out Of Scope

- Registry design
- Multi-agent orchestration or accountability composition
- Full governance platform claims
- Full FDO mapping
- Full cryptographic trust infrastructure
- Claims of standard adoption
- Claims of external deployment or production impact
- New experiments beyond the documented example/demo/acceptance path
- Broad performance, scalability, or collision-resistance claims

`artifact/artifact-pointer.md`, it should not appear in the paper as a factual
