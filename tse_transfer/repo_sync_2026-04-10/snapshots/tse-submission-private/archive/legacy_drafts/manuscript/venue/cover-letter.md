## Draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript on `Execution Evidence and Operation
Accountability Profile v0.1`, a deliberately bounded artifact for representing
and independently checking one operation accountability statement in an
FDO-based agent setting.

The submission is method-centric and artifact-centric. Its contribution is not
a broad governance framework or a large deployment study. Instead, it presents
a compact profile, a JSON Schema, a profile-aware validator exposed through
`agent-evidence validate-profile <file>`, a controlled example set, and a
runnable single-path demo. The accompanying public artifact repository includes
status, acceptance, and handoff materials so that readers and reviewers can
inspect the package against explicit evidence files.

We believe the manuscript may be of interest to venues that value reproducible
software engineering artifacts, bounded verification mechanisms, and concrete
research objects that can be independently reviewed. We have aimed to keep the
claims conservative and aligned with the repository-supported evidence.

Thank you for your consideration.
