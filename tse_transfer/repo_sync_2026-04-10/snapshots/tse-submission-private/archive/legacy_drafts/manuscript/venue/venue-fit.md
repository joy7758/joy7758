## Likely Fit

This work is best framed for venues that value:

- artifact-centered software engineering contributions
- reproducibility and evaluable research artifacts
- method papers with a clearly bounded technical scope
- runtime evidence, provenance, verification, or accountability mechanisms presented conservatively

## Likely Misfit

This work is not well aligned with venues expecting:

- broad empirical deployment studies
- large-scale benchmarking
- full governance frameworks or policy programs
- new cryptographic theory
- strong claims of ecosystem-wide adoption

## Positioning Guidance

Lead with the artifact and the validated path, not with a sweeping governance
story. The strongest pitch is that the package makes one accountability
statement concrete, checkable, and reproducible through a public repository,
examples, validator, demo, and acceptance materials.
