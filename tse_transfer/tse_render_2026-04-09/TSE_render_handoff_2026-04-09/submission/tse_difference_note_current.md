# Related Manuscript Note

This note explains why the present TSE submission and a related JSS witness
manuscript from the same broader research agenda are connected in subject
matter but are not duplicate manuscripts. The JSS witness manuscript is no
longer under review and should be understood as an inactive witness line
rather than as a concurrent submission. The artifact surface functions as
bounded supporting evidence for that argument rather than as the paper's
standalone contribution center.

## Roles In The Broader Research Agenda

- JSS witness role:
  the JSS line is the witness-oriented minimal implementation line for the
  underlying artifact. Its emphasis is the minimal profile, validator,
  controlled examples, runnable witness behavior, and artifact-oriented
  methodology.
- Current TSE flagship role:
  the present TSE line is the flagship manuscript for the problem-defining and
  verification-boundary argument. Its emphasis is why operation
  accountability should be treated as a first-class verification boundary, what
  that boundary does and does not establish, and how the current evidence-
  bounded validation framing should be interpreted.

## Scope Difference

- The JSS witness line stays close to the minimal profile, validator,
  controlled examples, and runnable witness needed to show that the mechanism
  can run and can be checked.
- The TSE flagship line frames the broader software engineering question around
  operation accountability as a verification boundary, while still keeping the
  empirical and validation boundary conservative.

## Contribution Difference

- The JSS witness line contributes the witness-oriented minimal implementation
  and its directly inspectable validator/demo path.
- The TSE flagship line contributes the problem definition, verification-
  boundary formulation, failure taxonomy, same-case comparison, and evidence-
  bounded validation framing built around that same broader research agenda.

## Evidence And Validation Difference

- The JSS witness line foregrounds the minimal profile, validator, controlled
  examples, and runnable witness as the evidence package.
- The TSE flagship line uses the same broader artifact agenda but foregrounds
  the bounded artifact surface only as supporting evidence for what the
  verification boundary means, how failure surfaces should be interpreted, how
  same-case comparison sharpens that boundary, and how the validation remains
  evidence-bounded rather than open-ended.

## Manuscript-Structure Difference

- The JSS witness line is organized around the minimal witness mechanism,
  validator path, controlled examples, and runnable specimen.
- The TSE flagship line is organized around the problem statement, scope
  boundary, profile design, validator and demo path, bounded evaluation,
  related work, threats to validity, and conclusion.

## Editor-Facing Conclusion

The two submissions are related because they arise from the same broader
research agenda, but they are not duplicates. They should not be understood as
earlier and later versions of the same paper. Instead, they address
different research questions, make materially different contributions, and use
the same broader research agenda for different editorial purposes. They should
not be understood as a concurrent submission pair.
